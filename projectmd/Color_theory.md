# ResuMatch - Color Theory & Theme Palettes

## 1. Brand & Platform Color Palette
ResuMatch branding focuses on trust, professionalism, and modern elegance.

- **Primary Brand Color**: `#1E3A8A` (Deep Slate Navy) - Symbolizes trust, reliability, and corporate standards.
- **Secondary Accent**: `#2563EB` (Royal Indigo) - Highlights interactive buttons, active steps, and progress bars.
- **Success & Action**: `#059669` (Emerald Green) - Indicates completed sessions, saved drafts, and successful downloads.
- **Background Neutral**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate Light Gray) - Clean background reducing eye strain.
- **Dark Text**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) - Optimal readability and contrast.
